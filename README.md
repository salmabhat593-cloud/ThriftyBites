# ThriftyBites 
AI-Based Smart Grocery Cart Generator and Price Comparator
ThriftyBites

About the Project:

ThriftyBites is a project developed to help people make better food choices while managing their budget. The main idea behind this project is to make food planning easier, more affordable, and smarter.

I created this project because many people, especially students and people living away from home, have a limited budget and find it difficult to decide what food items to buy. Prices of the same products can also be different on different platforms. ThriftyBites helps solve this problem by generating a smart food cart based on the user's budget and preferences.

Problem:

Planning food within a limited budget can be difficult. People often spend more money than expected or buy food without properly comparing prices. It can also be difficult to choose food that is both affordable and suitable for their needs.

Solution:

ThriftyBites helps users by suggesting food items according to their budget and requirements. The project focuses on creating a smart and affordable food cart and comparing prices so that users can make better decisions.

The aim is not only to save money but also to help users plan their food in a more organized way.

Main Features

- Smart food cart generation
- Budget-based food suggestions
- Food price comparison
- Personalized food recommendations
- Better planning based on user preferences
- Cost optimization
- Support for making smarter food choices

How the Project Works

First, the user provides their budget and food preferences. Based on this information, the system suggests suitable food items. The prices of different options can then be compared to help the user choose an affordable option.

The final goal is to generate a food cart that fits the user's budget and requirements.
